\section{Training and Implementation Details}
\label{app:training_details}

Table~\ref{tab:training_configuration} summarizes the two-stage configuration
for Baseline, VSP-Proj, and VSP. Baseline uses only the autoregressive
language-modeling objective. VSP-Proj adds projector-level alignment in both
stages, whereas VSP additionally aligns the final decoder-block image-token
states during instruction tuning. Both structural objectives use the same
frozen visual reference supplied to the projector, extracted from the current
preprocessed image. There is no direct CKA supervision at intermediate decoder
blocks.

\paragraph{CKA implementation.}
Corresponding visual-token rows are selected before centering. Each feature
channel is centered over the selected tokens of that image; images are never
pooled into a shared Gram matrix. The centered feature matrices are
Frobenius-normalized before forming their token--token Gram matrices, with no
per-token L2 normalization. Both diagonal and off-diagonal entries are retained.
Gram products and reductions are evaluated in FP32 with autocast disabled;
the TF32 setting remains enabled for eligible matrix multiplications.
For Gram matrices $K_X$ and $K_Y$, numerical stabilization uses
\begin{equation}
    d=\sqrt{\max\!\left(
        \lVert K_X\rVert_F^2\lVert K_Y\rVert_F^2,\,10^{-8}
    \right)}.
\end{equation}
The normalized inner product is clamped to $[0,1]$, and the per-image loss is
$1-\operatorname{CKA}$, without a nonzero tolerance margin. Feature normalization
floors its denominator at the smallest positive normal FP32 value. A zero Gram
matrix therefore produces CKA $=0$, not an excluded sample; the implementation
does not explicitly filter non-finite samples.

\paragraph{Token masks and reduction.}
Projector alignment uses all encoder patch features. Final-state alignment uses
the corresponding image-token positions retained after sequence truncation;
samples with fewer than two such positions do not contribute. The final-state
loss is the arithmetic mean over eligible samples in each micro-batch, and is
zero when there are none. Image-preprocessing padding is included in training
CKA, unlike the valid-region masks used in the diagnostic analyses.
The instruction-tuning data contain 40,688 text-only samples. These retain
language-modeling supervision and are excluded from final-state CKA. In the
current implementation, their dummy-image tensors still pass through the vision
encoder and contribute to projector CKA; the two CKA terms therefore do not use
identical sample populations in mixed batches.

\paragraph{Gradient paths.}
The visual reference $V$ is detached. Projector outputs $Z$ and final-block
outputs $H_{\mathrm{img}}^{(L)}$ remain differentiable. Thus,
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{proj}}$ updates the projector, while
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{final}}$ can update both the decoder and the projector.
No additional feature-matching module is introduced.
